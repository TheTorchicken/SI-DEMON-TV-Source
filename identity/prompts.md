# Exact generation prompts

Built-in image_gen was used. The first exploration was refined into the standalone emblem below; the final emblem then served as the icon reference. No API/CLI fallback was used.

## Logo

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: the supplied red horned car image. Turn it into a REAL GRAPHIC LOGO, preserving the red demonic car identity but simplifying it heavily. Keep the frontal symmetry, two red roof horns, intelligent narrow white headlight eyes and Ultra Red color. Change the car to the recognizable front of the refreshed Tesla Model 3: a very low smooth GRILLE-LESS nose, slender straight headlights, smooth rounded hood, subtle lower black intake only. Remove the large sports-car grille, aggressive side vents and overly sculpted supercar bumper. Make the entire mark a small set of crisp filled geometric shapes with subtle red tonal planes, NOT a photorealistic rendering. Remove every glow, halo, floor, shadow, environmental light and background. The only visible pixels are the compact emblem, floating on actual transparent alpha. Square composition with emblem centered, wide enough silhouette but compact vertically, complete horns and outline inside generous margins. Bold clean silhouette and broad negative spaces suitable for app icon downscaling. No text, no letters, no badge, no background rectangle, no Tesla T, no extra symbols. Premium original automotive logo of a red demon-like superintelligent Tesla, not an esports mascot.

## Mac app icon

Preserve that exact car model, front-facing smooth grille-less red nose, two curved red roof horns, angular focused silver-white headlight eyes, dark windshield and red tonal geometry. Build a square 1024x1024 Mac app icon: a finely crafted graphite #14161B rounded-square tile with a subtle dark bevel and softly polished edges. Outside the rounded tile is actual transparent alpha; tile occupies about 88% of canvas with uniform surrounding transparent padding. Place the red Tesla demon emblem centered on this tile, fitting its complete horns, mirrors and bumper comfortably inside the tile, with clear space on all four sides. Adapt the emblem to a BOLD SMALL-SIZE ICON: remove minor panel highlights and thin decorative lines; keep the main silhouette, hood, windshield, horns and clear bright headlight eyes. Saturated Ultra Red, dark crimson, graphite, restrained silver-white. Premium automotive software, precise and intelligent, not a cartoon mascot. Crisp clean contours and restrained dimensional material, no neon aura, no flames, no extraneous circuitry, no extra symbols, no text, no letters, no Tesla T, no wordmark. One icon only, no presentation board or multiple variants.
